{\LARGE\bfseries\color{navy} SONIC bench test: brief results}\par\vspace{3pt}

\textbf{Setup.} Computer $\to$ Audient iD14 $\to$ Edifier speaker $\to$ Earthworks M50 $\to$ microphone input 1. Four saved recordings at 96\,kHz; approximately 10\,cm separation (operator reported). This is an offline analysis.

\textbf{Timing.} All 40 sweeps were accepted. Pooled median application roundtrip: \textbf{38.307\,ms}; observed range: \textbf{38.240--38.375\,ms}. The two stream medians differed by 0.125\,ms.

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{TimingSummary.pdf}
\captionof{figure}{Top: waveform delay estimates for two stream starts. Bottom: matching strength; every probe exceeded the 0.35 threshold. Timing probes were separate 100\,Hz--20\,kHz sweeps, not SONIC tones.}
\end{center}

\textbf{Meaning.} Delay was repeatable over these short runs. It includes computer/interface buffering and the acoustic chain. The observed spread does not establish calibrated sub-millisecond accuracy, isolated speaker delay or neural latency.

\newpage
{\LARGE\bfseries\color{navy} Frequency response}\par\vspace{3pt}

\textbf{SONIC sounds.} Two 84-second blocks used a local reconstruction of the paper's 14 Sheep E frequencies (2--27\,kHz): 10\,ms pips, 2\,ms ramps, 16,800 pips total. These are not the original SONIC audio files.

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{FrequencyResponse.png}
\captionof{figure}{Top: screened sweep-response bands. Bottom: SONIC pip-component medians relative to 2\,kHz; whiskers show within-group 5th--95th percentiles, not confidence intervals.}
\end{center}

\textbf{Finding.} All 14 commanded components appear in both microphone recordings. Levels are uneven: 17--19.5\,kHz was about 15--22\,dB below 2\,kHz; 20.9--25.8\,kHz was 30--40\,dB below. These uncorrected full-chain recordings do not establish calibrated SPL, speaker-only response or distortion.

\textbf{Next.} Fix the geometry, calibrate microphone/SPL, test separated tones, and validate timing with an independent electrical reference.
