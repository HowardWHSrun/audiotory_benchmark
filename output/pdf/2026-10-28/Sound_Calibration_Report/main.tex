{\Large\bfseries Sound Calibration: Quick Results}\\
{\small Rice Auditory Benchmarking \quad | \quad 28 October 2026}

\textbf{Setup.} An Audient iD14 played 10 ms tone pips; an Earthworks M50 microphone recorded them approximately 10 cm from the speaker. A REED R8090 at 114 dB provided the reference.

\textbf{Correction.} We lowered the playback level of stronger frequencies so the recorded sound components were closer in level. In two tests, the range across the 14 frequency-group medians was \textbf{1.13 dB} and \textbf{0.77 dB}.
