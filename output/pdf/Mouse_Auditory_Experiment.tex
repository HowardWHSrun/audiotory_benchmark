{\LARGE\bfseries Mouse auditory experiment}\par
{\color{muted}A first plan \hfill October 8, 2026}\par

We plan the sound setup for mice using our lab's probes, with the aim of reusing the sound equipment for sedated sheep. SONIC tested awake sheep; our mouse state and tone settings remain open. \sonicref{6.6, p.13}

\topic{1. Choose the sounds}
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose the frequency, duration, loudness, and gap between tones.
\item \textbf{SONIC:} initially explored 1.4--32 kHz; benchmark tones were 10 ms sine waves with 2 ms fades at each end. \sonicref{6.1--6.2, p.11}
\item \textbf{SONIC:} random frequencies, balanced within each block, with no silence between tones. \sonicref{6.1, p.11}
\item Save the sequence before playback, as SONIC did. \sonicref{6.2, p.11}
\end{itemize}

\topic{2. Set up the speaker}
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose sound equipment with later sheep use in mind; the appendix explains the proposed parts.
\item \textbf{SONIC:} NI USB-6361 output at 192 kHz, TDT SA1 amplifier, and MF1 free-field speaker; about 80 dB SPL at sheep-ear distance. \sonicref{6.2, p.11}
\item \textbf{Our plan:} use the proposed shopping list on the next page, keep speaker placement fixed, and measure each tone with a calibrated microphone at the mouse-ear position.
\end{itemize}

\topic{3. Keep everything on one timeline}
\begin{itemize}
\item Use our lab's probes. Confirm the recording system's timing input so it can record the tone markers alongside the neural signals.
\item \textbf{SONIC:} recorded electrical sound output and trial-start markers; shared clock references aligned these with Connexus brain recordings. \sonicref{6.2, p.11}
\item \textbf{Our additions:} synchronize a microphone recording to check actual sound arrival. Align video frame times too if we add behavior video.
\end{itemize}

\topic{Why the speaker, amplifier, and power unit?}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{MF2-M speaker:} one speaker plays tones through the air. TDT lists a 1--80 kHz range, including SONIC's 1.4--32 kHz band. This broad range is useful for a shared mouse/sheep setup. Actual output still needs measuring.
\item \textbf{SA1 amplifier:} turns the NI waveform into power that drives the speaker. TDT explicitly supports SA1 with MF2; SONIC also used SA1.
\item \textbf{ZB1PS power unit:} SA1 needs TDT's zBus power. This chassis supplies it and houses the amplifier.
\end{itemize}

\topic{What changes when we move to sheep?}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Intended reuse:} speaker, amplifier, power unit, NI, computer, and sound recorder. We use our lab's probes.
\item \textbf{Set up and measure again:} change mounts, distance, and angle; calibrate each tone at the sheep-ear position and recheck sound arrival and recorder timing.
\item \textbf{Check the microphone first:} the kit's stated range starts at 2 kHz. SONIC's 1.4 kHz tones, or other lower sheep tones, need a suitable alternative microphone.
\item \textbf{Choose settings separately:} our sheep will be sedated; SONIC's were awake. Reusing equipment does not establish the same brain responses or response windows.
\end{itemize}
