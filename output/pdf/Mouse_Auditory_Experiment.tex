{\LARGE\bfseries Mouse auditory experiment}\par
{\color{muted}A first plan \hfill October 8, 2026}\par

We want to play short tones to a mouse and record its brain responses. SONIC tested awake sheep; our mouse state and tone settings remain open. \sonicref{6.6, p.13}

\topic{1. Choose the sounds}
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose the frequency, duration, loudness, and gap between tones.
\item \textbf{SONIC:} initially explored 1.4--32 kHz; benchmark tones were 10 ms sine waves with 2 ms fades at each end. \sonicref{6.1--6.2, p.11}
\item \textbf{SONIC:} random frequencies, balanced within each block, with no silence between tones. \sonicref{6.1, p.11}
\item Save the sequence before playback, as SONIC did. \sonicref{6.2, p.11}
\end{itemize}

\topic{2. Set up the speaker}
\begin{itemize}
\item Plan as though we need to assemble the whole sound setup.
\item \textbf{SONIC:} NI USB-6361 output at 192 kHz, TDT SA1 amplifier, and MF1 free-field speaker; about 80 dB SPL at sheep-ear distance. \sonicref{6.2, p.11}
\item \textbf{Our plan:} compare the equipment on the next page, keep speaker placement fixed, and measure each tone with a calibrated microphone at the mouse-ear position.
\end{itemize}

\topic{3. Keep everything on one timeline}
\begin{itemize}
\item Schedule each tone with a timing pulse and record it alongside the neural signals.
\item \textbf{SONIC:} recorded electrical sound output and trial-start markers; shared clock references aligned these with Connexus brain recordings. \sonicref{6.2, p.11}
\item \textbf{Our additions:} synchronize a microphone recording to check actual sound arrival. Align video frame times too if we add behavior video.
\end{itemize}

{\LARGE\bfseries Equipment we need}\par
{\color{muted}Mouse setup: building from scratch}\par

Xiaorong forwarded Tinghan's equipment list. We will use it to compare options; access to those items still needs confirming.

\topic{1. Play the tones}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Candidate path:} computer $\rightarrow$ timed sound output (DAC/DAQ) $\rightarrow$ ED1 driver $\rightarrow$ ES1 speaker.
\item \textbf{ES1:} plays sound through the air. Its intended range starts at 4 kHz; SONIC's lowest tones fall below that range. \href{https://www.tdt.com/docs/hardware/ec1-es1-electrostatic-speaker/}{\equipref{TDT speaker specs}}
\item \textbf{ED1:} also needs zBus power, such as ZB1PS, and the matching speaker cable. It does not generate the tones. \href{https://www.tdt.com/docs/hardware/ed1-electrostatic-speaker-driver/}{\equipref{TDT driver specs}}
\item \textbf{Other choices:} EC1 delivers sound through tubing. Peerless XT25TG30-04 and Scan-Speak R2004/602200 need a conventional amplifier; check their usable frequency range before choosing.
\end{itemize}

\topic{2. Measure the sound}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Avisoft option:} the complete UltraSoundGate 116Hm kit includes a CM16/CMPA microphone and preamplifier. The 116Hm alone is the recording interface. \href{https://avisoft.com/ultrasoundgate/116hm/}{\equipref{Avisoft kit details}}
\item \textbf{B\&K option:} the 4939 microphone needs a compatible preamplifier, polarization supply, and recorder. \href{https://www.bksv.com/-/media/literature/Product-Data/bp1851.ashx}{\equipref{4939 datasheet}}
\item Either path needs calibration records and a sound-level reference. Check each tone at the planned ear position.
\end{itemize}

\topic{3. Record the timing and brain signals}
\begin{itemize}
\item Choose the neural recorder and headstage, then match its event inputs to the tone-output system.
\item Record tone markers in both sound and neural data. Check actual sound arrival and clock drift; a shared marker alone does not make two clocks identical.
\item Include cables, power, fixed mounts, and data storage. Add a camera only if behavior is needed; align its frame times too.
\item A soundproof box is not required by current team direction. Record background sound and the setup geometry.
\end{itemize}

\topic{What else goes with these?}
\begin{itemize}
\item ES1/EC1 use ED1; MF1 uses SA1. Either TDT route needs zBus power. Confirm supplied speaker cables and stands before adding extra charges.

\item NI and AD3 are output alternatives. AD3 sequence length and tone-marker timing need bench checks before use in the final setup.
\end{itemize}
