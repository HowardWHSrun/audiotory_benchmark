Sound construction and limits

These are synthetic sine waves for a human speaker check. The 14-frequency list comes from SONIC bioRxiv v1, Section 6.1; the six-frequency subset, ascending repeating order, 96 kHz playback, and raised-cosine ramp shape are local choices. Pips last 10 ms with 2 ms onset and offset ramps. This does not reproduce the original stimulus order.

Playback requires macOS to report Audient output at 96 kHz and the browser audio context to run at 96 kHz. The software cannot verify the physical mixer, speaker wiring, SPL, distortion, acoustic timing, or speaker bandwidth. This player is separate from the educational walkthrough and is not an animal-ready stimulus protocol.
