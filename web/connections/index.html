BEFORE RECORDING

A few things to check.

01

Set the mixer. Send microphone input 1 to Cue A, pan left, and assign Cue A to outputs 3/4. Keep mic monitoring out of the speaker mix.

02

Confirm the cable. The output 3L → Ain1 adapter is a proposal. Confirm the ECU SMA pinout, input impedance and safe levels with SpikeGadgets before connecting; then check continuity and clipping.

03

Check the bandwidth. The ECU samples analog at up to 30 kHz, so it cannot faithfully capture 17–27 kHz carriers. A pre-ECU envelope circuit needs suitable filtering and a measured delay; alternatively use faster synchronized acquisition.
