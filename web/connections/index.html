The missing connection

You said you do not yet have the cable from the iD14 to the ECU. This is one proposed three-part assembly; confirm compatibility before ordering.

  1. 01
    ¼-inch TRS breakout

    TRS male → two ¼-inch TS female sockets. Plug into iD14 3L; use the black tip / hot branch. Leave the red ring / cold branch open.

Before ordering or connecting: Confirm the ECU’s SMA center / shell assignment, input impedance, grounding, and safe signal levels with SpikeGadgets; then verify continuity and check for clipping. This cable assembly has not been tested on your setup.

Other cables to check on your bench

Your microphone, iD14, and speakers are already connected, but we have not inspected their exact cable routing. Check for the M50 XLR cable, the iD14 USB-C cable, and the speaker connections. Also check the ECU-to-MCU HDMI cable and the MCU-to-computer Ethernet cable; these may be included with the respective units. Confirm the required headstage cable and any HCU / commutator against your exact hardware.

BEFORE RECORDING

A few things to check.

01

Set the mixer. Send microphone input 1 to Cue A, pan left, and assign Cue A to outputs 3/4. Keep mic monitoring out of the speaker mix.

02

Confirm the cable. The output 3L → Ain1 adapter is a proposal. Confirm the ECU SMA pinout, input impedance and safe levels with SpikeGadgets before connecting; then check continuity and clipping.

03

Check the bandwidth. The ECU samples analog at up to 30 kHz, so it cannot faithfully capture 17–27 kHz carriers. A pre-ECU envelope circuit needs suitable filtering and a measured delay; alternatively use faster synchronized acquisition.
